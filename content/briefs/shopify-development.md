Status: proposal (November 21, 2026). Keywords are not in `docs/seo/keyword-map.md` until Nayab approves them.
Research: `docs/seo/research/shopify-keywords.csv`, `shopify-wordpress-serp-notes.md`, `shopify-wordpress-report.md`.

## 1. Primary keyword and intent

- Proposed primary: **shopify development agency** (100–1K US searches, Ads competition Low, top-of-page bid up to $63.26, Ahrefs KD 3).
- Current primary: shopify agency (1K–10K, Low, $32.27, KD 56). Keep it as a secondary.
- Intent: hire. The reader is a US store owner or brand manager who wants someone to build, fix or grow a Shopify store.
- Page type: service page (`seo.md` §10: 1,200+ words, process, deliverables, tech, pricing range, FAQ 6+, case study link).
- Target length: about 2,000 words.

## 5. What the top results cover, and the gap

- Top results: Shopify Partner Directory, Reddit threads, "best Shopify agencies" lists, Upwork/Toptal/Fiverr, and agency pages with DR 50–79 (Coalition, Shero Commerce, Netalico).
- Pages we opened: Shero Commerce hire page (2,260 words, FAQ, Service + FAQPage schema, no price), Coalition Shopify page (2,447 words, a $2,500 figure, Shopify Premier Partner badge, process, FAQ), shopifyconsultants.com (DR 0.2, ranks #6 for "shopify consultant": free store health check, process, apps built, FAQ).
- Gap for our page:
  - A clear price range for small and mid-size US stores (setup, redesign, custom work, monthly support).
  - A process with a timeline per step, and what happens in week 1.
  - Ownership: the client owns the store, theme code and app accounts after handover.
  - Direct work with one senior developer in a US-friendly time window, versus agencies and marketplaces.
  - A free Shopify store audit as the low-risk first step.
  - Real Shopify work from Nayab (case study link only if real and approved).
