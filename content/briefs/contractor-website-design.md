## 5. The gap this page fills

Contractor pages that rank tend to be a template gallery or a generic "we build websites" page. This page sells one idea: a site that wins estimate requests, plus the automation behind it. It adds an estimate form that sorts jobs by size, a lead-flow picture (form or call → job questions → site visit → CRM texts), an AI receptionist for calls on the job site, and an honest comparison of DIY builders, lead platforms (Angi, HomeAdvisor) and your own website. Competitor text is never copied.
