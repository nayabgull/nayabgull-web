## 1. Primary keyword and intent

- Proposed primary: **wordpress development agency** (100–1K US searches, Ads competition Low, top-of-page bid up to $42.98, Ahrefs KD 5). Already a secondary on this page.
- Current primary: wordpress agency (1K–10K, Low, $29.92, KD 43). Keep it as a secondary.
- Intent: hire. The reader is a US small-business owner or marketing lead who needs a new WordPress site, a rebuild or custom features.
- Page type: service page (`seo.md` §10: 1,200+ words, process, deliverables, tech, pricing range, FAQ 6+, case study link).
- Target length: about 2,000 words.

## 3. Entities to cover

WordPress, block editor (Gutenberg), full site editing, themes and child themes, custom theme, plugins, custom plugin, Advanced Custom Fields, custom post types, WooCommerce, Elementor, page speed and Core Web Vitals, caching and CDN, managed WordPress hosting, staging site, backups, security updates, PHP version, SEO plugins, forms and CRM integration, accessibility (WCAG), headless WordPress, Next.js.

## 4. Questions to answer (People Also Ask, Google US)

- What is a WordPress agency?

- How much does WordPress development cost?
- Is WordPress outdated in 2026? (appears on the agency, hire and maintenance SERPs)
- Which WordPress design agency is the best in the USA? (answer with selection criteria, not a fake ranking)

## 5. What the top results cover, and the gap

- Top results: partner directories (WP VIP, WP Engine), Clutch, Reddit lists, "best WordPress development agencies" posts, and US agencies with DR 59–81 (WebDevStudios, Bluetext, Orbit Media, Human Made).
- Pages we opened: Pronto Marketing WordPress design page (3,370 words, $4,500–$15,000 builds, $99/month plans, money-back guarantee, Service schema), wordpressmaintenanceservice.com (DR 1.7: exact H1, monthly prices, free site check, FAQPage + Service schema).
- Gap for our page:
  - Price ranges for a small business site, a custom build and custom features, with what changes the price.
  - When WordPress is the right choice and when it is not (answers "Is WordPress outdated in 2026?").
  - Custom work explained in plain words: custom theme vs page builder, custom plugin, ACF fields.
  - Ownership and handover: hosting, admin access, code and licenses stay with the client.
  - Speed and security baked in, with a link to the care plan for after launch.
  - Direct work with one senior developer in a US-friendly time window.
